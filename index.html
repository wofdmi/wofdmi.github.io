Who We Are

About Word of Faith DMI

Word of Faith DMI is a Spirit-filled church committed to proclaiming the gospel of Jesus Christ and making disciples who impact their communities and the world.

Founded on the Word of God, we believe in the power of faith, the importance of community, and the transforming grace of Jesus Christ for every area of life.

📚

Word-based

Everything we do is grounded in Scripture.

🌟

Spirit-led

We pursue the fullness of the Holy Spirit.

❤

Family

A genuine community of love and belonging.

🌎

Mission

Reaching our city and the nations with Christ.
